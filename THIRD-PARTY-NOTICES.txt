4. The game
-----------

This mod is not made or supported by the game's developer.

The billboard's frame, name plate, letters and shadow are the mod's own pictures (embedded in
ShorelandsBillboard.dll), drawn for this mod in the game's style and with colours taken from the game's
stalls. No file of the game is included in this package.

While the game runs, the mod makes copies of a few of the game's own objects in memory: a lantern of the
tree house (twice for the posts, once for the screen's glow), a tree's ground shadow light, and it uses
the game's sprite material and status font. It hides five hedge bushes under the billboard and shows them
again when the billboard is removed. Nothing is written to the game's files.
